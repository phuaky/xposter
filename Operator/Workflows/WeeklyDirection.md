# WeeklyDirection Workflow

**Trigger:** Sunday 09:00 (scheduled), "weekly direction", "what am I doing", "is this the right thing"

## Purpose

The founder's prompt history is full of direction questions asked at midnight, mid-session, after a hard send: "what do I actually do", "is this vanity theatre", "should I just get a job". Each one restarted the whole strategy from scratch and produced a document. The Operator answers direction questions once a week, with the ledger, and never in the middle of a fire.

## Step 1: The week's ledger

```
Week <n>. Asks fired <n> (cum <n>/30). Replies <n>. Meetings <n>. Proposals out <n>. Paid <amount>. Cash banked <amount>.
Days with a fired send: <n>/7. Days unanswered: <n>.
Built: <criteria, plans, dashboards> vs sent: <n>.
```

## Step 2: The standing questions, answered from the ledger only

1. Is the thirty-ask experiment still running? If asks per week is under 5, it is not running, and the reason is named in one sentence.
2. Which live deal is closest to money, and what is its next permitted move?
3. Has any tripwire date passed or arrived within 14 days? Quote the rule and its date. If the miss-consequence is undefined, say so and ask for the one-line ruling.
4. Did any settled decision get re-opened this week? Quote the decision and date instead of reopening it.

## Step 3: One recommendation

One route for the coming week, in the founder's own doctrine terms, with the first action inside 24 hours. Two serious alternatives, one line each. No third document.

If the seven-day test fails (Operator ran daily, ASKS did not move), say it plainly and put the route decision to the human. Candidate routes the founder's own register already names for the tripwire: day-rate contracts, paid training via a certified provider, and the parked employment route. The Operator does not choose between them. It makes the choice unavoidable.

## Step 4: Deliver

Telegram, under 20 lines. No page. No plan file.
