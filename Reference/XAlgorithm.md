## TweepCred System

| Score | Status |
|-------|--------|
| 0-64 | **SEVERELY LIMITED** — Only 3 tweets get distribution |
| 65+ | Normal distribution |
| 100 | Maximum |

### If You Drop Below 0.65

1. Pause posting 48-72 hours
2. Delete problematic tweets
3. Limit to <5 follows/likes per hour
4. Focus only on high-quality replies
5. Rebuild over 2-4 weeks

## The Reply Revolution

1. **You replying to comments = 75x** (highest ROI action in entire algorithm)
2. **Getting replies = 13.5-27x** (design every post for this)
3. Reply chains boost all participants

### Execution

- Check your posts every 2-3 hours
- Reply to EVERY comment
- Even brief replies matter
- This single habit outweighs everything else

## Critical Timing Changes

### First 30 Minutes
- Engagement velocity in first 30 min = algorithmic destiny
- High early engagement → expanded distribution
- Low early engagement → suppressed visibility

### Author Diversity Penalty
- Consecutive posts from same account get downranked
- Space posts by 30-60 minutes minimum
- Don't batch-post content

### Posting Time Optimization
**NOW OBSOLETE** — Phoenix removed time-based ranking features entirely. Focus on content quality, not schedule.

## Nikita Bier Strategy (Still Valid from 2025)

Source: Nikita Bier (@nikitabier), Head of Product at X

### 6-Month Niche Strategy
- Pick ONE subject you know better than anyone
- Post ONE unexpected insight daily
- Keep posts under 5 sentences
- Maintain consistency for 6 months

> "If you stick to it, we will promote your account to others." — Nikita Bier

### Medium-Form Wins
> "The best content on X is neither short form nor long form. It's medium form: 1-2 paragraphs."

Threads REDUCE reactions. Medium-form single posts perform better.

### Canvas for Comments
> "A good post just provokes discussion; nothing else. The post is just a canvas for the comments."

Don't deliver complete thoughts. End with questions. Optimize for conversation.

### Reply-Guy Growth
All top posters started obscure, replied for YEARS in obscurity. Consistency beats viral moments.

### Humor as PR Shield
> "You can survive any PR crisis if you're funny."

## Risk Management

**Never risk:**
- Offensive content (1 report = -369x)
- Controversial topics (until TweepCred established)
- Links in main post (severe penalty)
- Consecutive posts (author diversity penalty)

**Always:**
- Include media (2-3x boost)
- End with discussion prompt (triggers replies)
- Monitor for negative signals
- Protect TweepCred above 0.65
